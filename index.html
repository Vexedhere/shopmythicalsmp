Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you choose a rank.

How long does my rank last?

Ranks on this page are presented as 30-day packages. Confirm that the matching Tebex package and server commands are configured for the same duration before purchasing.

Can I renew my rank?

You can purchase the package again. Whether time adds onto your current rank or starts a new period depends on the server's permission and command setup.

When will I receive my rank?

Delivery depends on Tebex processing and the server's configured commands. Make sure you enter your Minecraft username correctly at checkout.

What if I chose the wrong username?

Don't make another purchase immediately. Contact the server team through its official support channel and provide your Tebex transaction details privately.

Are the prices final?

The prices shown here are display prices only. Tebex checkout is the final source for package price, currency, taxes, and available payment methods.

Do I need to buy a rank to play?

These ranks are optional purchases. Don't buy anything unless you want the listed perks and are comfortable with the final checkout amount.
